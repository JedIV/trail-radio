Privacy policy

The sounds stay on your device. Your settings do too.

Who provides Trail Radio

Trail Radio is provided by John Dougherty. Questions about this policy can be sent to john.e.dougherty@gmail.com.

Data collected by the app

Trail Radio does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. It does not access your microphone, location, contacts, or health data.

Local settings

Your volume and sound levels are saved locally on your device so the app can remember your mix. These settings are not sent to us. You can change them in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored settings.

Sound generation

Audio is generated on your device. Playback does not download or stream recordings, and works without internet reception.

Support email

If you email support, your email address, message, and any information you choose to provide are used to respond to your request. Email is handled by your email provider and the support mailbox provider. Please send only the information needed to describe your question.

Apple and this website

Apple may separately process App Store downloads and diagnostics under its own privacy policies and your device settings. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes website requests under its own policies. This website adds no analytics or tracking scripts. The app does not contact this website during audio playback.

Changes

If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.
