OFFLINE · ON YOUR DEVICE

A little pocket prairie.

Warm, rolling wind. Quiet hoofbeats. A creaking wagon and birds in the distance.

Trail Radio is a simple iPhone and iPad ambience app. Its sounds are generated on your device, so playback needs no internet connection. Adjust each layer, choose an optional sleep timer, and keep listening with the screen locked.

Support & feedback

Contact John Dougherty at john.e.dougherty@gmail.com.

For a problem report, include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and what happened. Please don’t include passwords or other sensitive information.

Getting started

  1. Tap Take the Trail to start. Tap Rest a While to pause.
  2. Adjust Volume, Wind, Hoofbeats, Wagon Creaks, and Distant Birds to make your mix.
  3. Leave the sleep timer off for continuous playback, or choose a duration.

If you can’t hear anything

Check the app volume, your device’s media volume, and whether audio is being sent to headphones or another speaker. Then try pausing and playing again. If the app displays an error, include its text when contacting support.

Private by design

No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Sound settings stay on your device. Read the privacy policy for details.

Use a comfortable, low listening level. Trail Radio is an ambience app and does not provide medical treatment.
